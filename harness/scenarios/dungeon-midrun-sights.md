# Dungeon mid-run sights

A cultivator starts a managed rank-1 dungeon. The first wake is still the wounded traveler, with 扶起伤者, 分一口水, and 拿走袋子. Later wakes are Chinese and offer three or four choices among a fork, a relic, a fellow cultivator, and a contested pouch. The run still stops at three wakes.

By the time the job ends, the travelogue and the six-tick digest contain a 见闻 sentence. If a guardian fight was won, that fight's last beat, the travelogue, and the digest share one 带出何物 line.

An Eight Wastes expedition shows the same kind of Chinese wake, three or four choices, and writes a short sighting as the depth advances.
