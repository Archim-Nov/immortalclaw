# Party expedition social

Form a party of five in the city. Fulfill `ask-favor:escort-gate` on the way, say `rendezvous-at-gate`, and enter `enter-dungeon:rank-1:balanced` together. Companions stay idle.

At step 3, `sharedDungeon.partyWake.kind` is `tend-wound`. The wounded companion still sees `dungeon-wake-decision:tend-companion`. Enough votes for 伸手压伤 clear the wake and leave 替我挡了一剑.

At step 6, one `take-shortcut` vote does not lock. After the timeout the purse is unchanged, grudge stays 0, and someone owes 把灵材让给我.

At step 8, three `seize-treasure` votes lock. The first seizer's purse gains 10. Grudge with a bystander is at least 3 and still under 嫌隙.

When the job ends, both travelogues and both digests contain a paired 上回 line for the wound, the yielded stones, and the seized find. The escort and the doorway meeting are quoted once each.

A cultivator who entered alone still gets the step-3 wake with 扶起伤者, 分一口水, and 拿走袋子.

On 乙 the party window is ten ticks. Step 6 is the floor's favor, not the shortcut. `cover-mate` in 雷泽残阵 leaves 断法的时候把丹递给我 and raises affinity. `take-opening` adds 8 stones and grudge 3, still short of 嫌隙. A solo 乙 step 6 is the thin chance, and step 8 is the quarrel. A search on the seventh step names 雷索客 and 麻符修.
