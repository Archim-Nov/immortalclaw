# Residence sink and a captured camp

A cultivator standing in their own cave can rebuild it and buy furniture on the same residence they already own. Safe-state rebuilds cost 8000, 36000, 160000, and 720000, then triple. Most furniture reaches 绝品. Filling the catalog costs more than twenty million stones. The bonuses stop at six meditation progress, three heal ticks, twelve qi, 28% off crafting, six hosting affinity, and 24 local reputation from that cave. One 绝品 piece stays under those ceilings. Taking a piece down does not refund stones or pay the reputation again.

A border camp another sect still holds says 邻宗占在本州 in `sectLife.veins[].materials`. After the member's sect takes it, that sentence says the yield belongs to the sect. An unowned camp does not use the neighbor sentence.

Action cards, the travelogue, and the act result stay in Chinese and do not name the action id.
