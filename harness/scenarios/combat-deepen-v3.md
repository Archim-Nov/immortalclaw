# Combat Deepen v3

Chat up a rival in the same city. Both travelogues name that person and say 宿敌.

Fill a party, queue a reserve, and `party-rotate`. The person who steps in and the person who steps out each have a note naming the swap. `leave-party-reserve` notes the leader as well.

Give a healing pill to someone who is wounded. Accepting it says the pill can ease the wound. It stays in their pack until they use it or someone tends them.

Set an injury and try `enter-dungeon`. It is refused. `tend-wound:<id>` from someone in the same place lowers the injury, names both people, and leaves a favor. `use-healing-pill` and an idle tick also lower it.

A 丙 rank 1 guardian is still one enemy. A 丙 rank 3 guardian names a 从者 beside the elite. A solo 炼气-like fighter still rarely wins rank 1. A mixed party of five does not win every rank 3 fight, and aggressive wins less often than balanced.
