# Companion shared report

Stand at a dungeon gate with two idle party members. Say one sentence, tend a wound if someone is hurt, then `enter-dungeon:rank-1:balanced` and let the job finish.

Each companion's `dungeonExpedition.lastReport.combatRounds` matches the leader's: enemy, outcome, role, and skill names. `sharedLeaderId` is the leader. `sharedDungeon` is gone. One travel note on each of them names a role, a skill with 使出, the spoken sentence, and the tended wound.

A city duel between two party members writes the same `combatRounds` on both `recentCombats`. A third member in that city has a 切磋 note that names a skill.
